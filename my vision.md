My Vision.

40 minutes from Marbella heading towards Ronda, we find Cartajima as the highest entrance to the Genal Valley. Only 15 minutes from Ronda, this valley presents unique landscape characteristics due to its chestnut forests.

The estate has 78,000 m2 and has a special protection status, as it is located in this valley of such singular landscape. That is why this license for the action project is so valuable.

I have had to wait more than 20 years since I bought the estate to achieve a political alignment between the Town Hall, the Junta de Andalucía (Regional Government), and the Diputación de Málaga (Provincial Council), which made this license possible. Unfortunately, it is now too late for me. Over all these years, my family and personal circumstances have changed, and I am no longer in a position to develop my vision.

It is an ecotourism complex composed of 10 small houses, a large central building, storage, orchard... The idea is that due to the size of the estate, you can build the 10 houses far enough apart so that each tenant has a complete sense of intimacy and connection with nature. For me, this is the great luxury of the future.

In the large central house, events, courses, meetings... can be held, so that tenants have a space where they can connect with other people.

The estate has everything needed to build one of the most special places in Málaga.

-	Water supply from the village, as well as a spring that I have not seen dry up even in the driest years. On the southern boundary, the Arrollo Blanco (White Stream) has waterfalls and pools where you can refresh yourself. On a walk, you reach the Genal River and its crystal-clear water pools.
-	An electricity pole on the estate itself from which electrical supply can be connected for the entire complex.
-	South-facing orientation and a moderate slope.
-	The entire project is located on 2 of the 4 plots of the estate, leaving two other plots available for other uses.
-	Its location 40 minutes from Marbella and 15 from Ronda, the two most tourist points in Málaga. (It is expected that in the coming years the Ronda-Marbella road will be widened, which would notably reduce this time)
